## COURSE OVERVIEW

This course walks through the books of Daniel and Revelation using the Phototheology method: seeing Christ as the central theme of all prophecy, the sanctuary as the interpretive master key, and typological patterns as God's teaching language. Every prophetic chain points to Jesus — His earthly ministry, His heavenly ministry, and His return.

**Core Principle:** "The greatest sign we can give to an evil and adulterous generation is NOT the prophecy of Revelation 13 or Daniel 7 — it is the death, burial, and resurrection of Jesus Christ." All prophecy is a means to this end.

**Method:** "Jesus-esis" — reading Scripture through the lens of Christ as ultimate Author. Not just exegesis (what the human author intended) but what the divine Author embedded in multi-dimensional layers. "God has a thousand different ways to reveal the same truth."
