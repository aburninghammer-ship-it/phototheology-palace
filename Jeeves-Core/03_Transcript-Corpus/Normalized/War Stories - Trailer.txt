So it's Friday night. I'm a young college student at this time. I'm attending a party at another university. I'm making my way through this party... ...and I notice that there's this guy standing a distance away from me, and he's just staring at me. I don't pay it any attention. I keep doing my thing. Every now and then, I look up and the guy is a little bit closer--still staring at me. I still don't pay it any attention. At one point, I'm talking to someone when suddenly, I hear these words behind me: "What did you say?"

I turned around and it's this guy--standing right in my face. I turned back around to look at the guy that I was talking to to see if he was talking to him by any chance and then I turned back to look at him. And I was like "Are you talking to me?" And he was like "Yeah! What did you say?" And I was like "I didn't say anything to you." And as soon as I said that, this guy takes his fist and *BOOM*--right in my face. My head snaps back. And I'm like-- --I don't respond. I look around and I suddenly notice that he's got his friends surrounding me -- I estimate probably 20 of them.

And I'm thinking to myself "Things are about to go down." "This is not good."
