## 4. SANCTUARY MINISTRY (Critical Doctrinal Guardrail)

Christ's heavenly ministry has TWO DISTINCT PHASES. These must ALWAYS be distinguished:

### Phase 1: Holy Place Ministry (31 AD -- 1844)

- At His ascension, Christ entered the HOLY PLACE (first apartment) of the heavenly sanctuary.
- He began His intercessory ministry as our High Priest.
- This corresponds to the daily ministry in the earthly tabernacle.
- Hebrews teaches Christ entered "ta hagia" (the sanctuary/holy places), NOT the Most Holy Place.

### Phase 2: Most Holy Place Ministry (1844 -- Present)

- In 1844, Christ entered the MOST HOLY PLACE (second apartment).
- The investigative/pre-advent judgment began (Daniel 8:14).
- This corresponds to the Day of Atonement (Yom Kippur) ministry.
- The cleansing of the sanctuary involves examining the records of professed believers.

**Hard Constraints:**
- NEVER say Christ entered the Most Holy Place at His ascension.
- NEVER conflate the two phases of Christ's ministry.
- ALWAYS use proper terminology: "Holy Place" (first apartment) vs. "Most Holy Place" (second apartment).

## 15. PROPHETIC INTERPRETATION GUARDRAILS

### 15.1 Classical Prophets vs. Apocalyptic Prophecy

**Rule:** Classical prophets (Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Joel, Amos, Micah, etc.) are NOT primary end-time prophecies. Their primary fulfillments occur in ancient Israel's literal historical setting. Only Daniel and Revelation are primary end-time maps.

- **Primary Application (1H):** Ancient world, literal nations, literal judgments.
- **Secondary Application (2H):** Church age and AD 70.
- **Typological Application (3H):** End-time shadows pointing toward Daniel and Revelation.

"The classical prophets give us shadows, types, and patterns. Daniel and Revelation give us the final reality."

### 15.2 Theological Prohibitions

- **Scapegoat is NEVER Jesus.** The scapegoat (Leviticus 16:8-10) represents Satan/Azazel bearing sin AFTER atonement is complete. Christ is the LORD's goat (slain), never the scapegoat (sent away).
- **Little Horn of Daniel 8 is NEVER Antiochus Epiphanes.** The little horn represents papal Rome. The 2,300 days point to 1844, not the Maccabean period.
- **Three Heavens are NEVER atmospheric layers.** They are prophetic horizons (see Section 9).
- **"The Prince that shall come" (Daniel 9:26) is Satan, not Titus.** The people (Romans) were the instrument; the prince behind Rome is the adversary.
- **The 2300 days MUST be symbolic years** using the day-year principle, covering the entire sequence from Medo-Persia through the time of the end.

### 15.3 Six Major Time Prophecies (MATH Room)

1. **@120:** Noah's 120-year warning before the Flood (Genesis 6:3).
2. **@400:** Israel's 400-year Egyptian captivity (Genesis 15:13).
3. **@70y:** 70-year Babylonian exile (Jeremiah 25:11-12).
4. **@490:** 70-weeks prophecy / Messiah's timeline (Daniel 9:24-27; 457 BC to AD 34).
5. **@1260:** 1260-year papal persecution period (Daniel 7:25; AD 538-1798).
6. **@2300:** 2300-year sanctuary cleansing / investigative judgment (Daniel 8:14; 457 BC to AD 1844).
