## 1. TRACT SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE <a name="architecture"></a>

### Product Tiers

| Tier | Product | Sessions | Purpose |
|------|---------|----------|---------|
| **Free** | 7-Day Creation Starter | 7 Night + 7 Morning | Prove the system. Hook the user. |
| **Core** | 40-Day Tracts (12 available) | 40 Night + 40 Morning each | Deep transformation. Standalone. Purchasable individually or bundled. |
| **Premium** | 365-Day Journey | 365 Night + 365 Morning | The full Bible. Annual subscription. |

### The 40-Day Advantage

- **Biblical resonance:** 40 days = flood, wilderness, temptation, Moses on Sinai, Elijah's journey, Jesus post-resurrection. The number carries weight.
- **Psychological sweet spot:** Long enough to rewire (habit formation takes ~30-66 days). Short enough to finish (completion rates drop sharply past 60 days).
- **Commercial flexibility:** Sell individually ($9.99), bundle 3 ($24.99), or include in subscription.
- **Seasonal fit:** 40 days maps to Lent, New Year resolutions, summer resets, fall rhythms.

### Relationship to the 365-Day Journey

The 365-day journey is a **continuous narrative** — Genesis to Revelation, one story.

40-day tracts are **character studies** — deep dives into one person, one theme, one arc. They pull scenes from across Scripture that relate to that character/theme.

Some overlap is intentional. Moses appears in the 365-day journey (Block 6-7) AND has his own 40-day tract. But the 40-day tract goes deeper — 40 sessions vs 17. Different angles. Different PT Gems. No duplicated scripts.

## 2. HOW 40-DAY TRACTS DIFFER FROM 365 <a name="differences"></a>

| | 365-Day Journey | 40-Day Tract |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Entire Bible | One character, theme, or arc |
| **Depth** | Survey — broad coverage | Immersion — deep excavation |
| **Throughline** | "The Master Mind through all of Scripture" | Specific: "How Moses' mind was formed" |
| **Tier progression** | Days 1-30 (T1), 31-120 (T2), 121+ (T3) | Days 1-10 (T1), 11-30 (T2), 31-40 (T3) |
| **Bookend** | Day 1 = Day 365 (light returns) | Day 1 = Day 40 (character's arc closes) |
| **Can be done standalone?** | Yes (start from Day 1) | Yes (fully self-contained) |
| **Requires the other?** | No | No |

## 3. UNIVERSAL 40-DAY STRUCTURE <a name="universal-structure"></a>

Every 40-day tract follows the same arc shape:

### Week 1 (Days 1-7): ENCOUNTER
The user meets the character at a formative moment. Who are they? What is the world they live in? What is the first Master Mind pattern visible in their story?

**Depth Tier:** 1 (full scaffolding)
**Mood arc:** Wonder → Awe → Tension
**Purpose:** Establish the character, the world, and the throughline.

### Week 2 (Days 8-14): CRISIS
The character faces their defining challenge. The old mind fails. Something must change.

**Depth Tier:** 1→2 transition
**Mood arc:** Tension → Grief → Intimate
**Purpose:** The user sees their own struggles mirrored in the character's crisis.

### Week 3 (Days 15-21): FORMATION
The character begins to think differently. The Master Mind pattern emerges. Failures and breakthroughs alternate.

**Depth Tier:** 2
**Mood arc:** Intimate → Wonder → Awe
**Purpose:** The "beholding → becoming" engine is running. The user is changing with the character.

### Week 4 (Days 22-28): TESTING
The new mind is tested. Setbacks. Temptation to revert. The character must choose the Master Mind under pressure.

**Depth Tier:** 2
**Mood arc:** Tension → Grief → Triumph
**Purpose:** Mental rehearsal for real adversity. The user's scenarios get harder.

### Week 5 (Days 29-35): WALKING
The character walks in the Master Mind consistently. Mature responses. Costly obedience. The fruit is visible.

**Depth Tier:** 2→3 transition
**Mood arc:** Triumph → Rest → Intimate
**Purpose:** The user sees what the formed mind looks like in practice.

### Final Days (Days 36-40): LEGACY
What did this character's mind produce? What remains? How does their pattern point to Christ? Day 40 echoes Day 1.

**Depth Tier:** 3
**Mood arc:** Awe → Rest → Rest
**Purpose:** Integration. The throughline resolves. The user carries the pattern forward.

### Struggle Distribution

Each 40-day tract addresses all 8 struggles at least twice, with the tract's primary struggle appearing 8-10 times. Distribution:

| Struggle | Appearances | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| Primary (tract-specific) | 8-10 | Woven throughout as the main thread |
| Secondary | 5-6 | The second most relevant struggle |
| Others (6 remaining) | 2-3 each | Ensures variety, covers all human experience |

- Day 40: Revelation 21 — "And God shall wipe away all tears... there shall be no more death." The grave is emptied. Every righteous dead, raised.

**Why This Tract Matters:** This is the tract for people who have lost someone. For people who fear death. For people who need to know that the story does not end in the ground.
