## 1. HOW THE THREE TRACTS DIFFER <a name="differences"></a>

| Dimension | Tract 1: Biblical Narrative | Tract 2: Human Experience | Tract 3: Cloud of Witnesses |
|-----------|---------------------------|--------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Organizing principle** | Chronological — Genesis to Revelation | Thematic — organized by struggle, virtue, and human need | Biographical — deep immersion in individual lives |
| **Movement** | Forward through history | Spiral — revisits themes at deeper levels | Relational — each character hands off to the next |
| **Scripture usage** | Sequential passages | Cross-biblical — pulls from everywhere | Concentrated — stays with one person's story for weeks |
| **Bookend** | Day 1: "Let there be light" / Day 365: same scene, new ears | Day 1: "Who am I?" / Day 365: "I am His" | Day 1: Adam alone in the garden / Day 365: the great multitude, no longer alone |
| **Best for** | First-time users wanting the whole Bible | Users in active struggle wanting targeted help | Users who learn through story and relationship |
| **Emotional arc** | Epic — sweeping narrative | Intimate — personal and surgical | Empathic — walking in another's shoes |
| **Repeat value** | Low (same story) | High (different struggles surface each year) | Medium (characters don't change, but you do) |

## 3. TRACT 2: THE HUMAN EXPERIENCE <a name="tract-2"></a>

**Subtitle:** "The Mind That Meets You Where You Are"

### Design Philosophy

Tract 1 asks: *What did God do?*
Tract 2 asks: *What do YOU need?*

This tract is organized around **12 human experiences** — not chronologically through Scripture, but thematically through the landscape of human need. Each theme gets a multi-week deep dive, pulling from all over the Bible. The user doesn't walk through history — they walk through themselves.

### The Spiral Principle

Tract 2 visits some themes more than once. Anxiety appears in Block 1 and returns in Block 9 at a deeper level. This is intentional. The first pass names the struggle. The second pass goes underneath it. The user who reaches Block 9 is not the same person who started Block 1.

### Bookend

**Day 1 — "Who Am I?"**
Scene: A mirror in a dark room. The user stands before it. The reflection is unclear — obscured, shifting. The Master Mind insight: "You cannot see yourself clearly yet. But you are seen."

**Day 365 — "I Am His"**
Scene: The same mirror. Same room. But now the reflection is steady. Not because the user has changed — because the light has. The Master Mind insight: "You were always this. You just couldn't see it until now."

### Quality Control Rules (Tract 2-Specific)

1. **No theme repeats its scene language** — if Block 2 uses "storm" for anxiety, Block 22 must use different imagery
2. **The spiral must deepen** — revisited themes at Tier 3 cannot use Tier 1 scaffolding
3. **Cross-references are encouraged** — Block 22 can callback to Block 2 explicitly: "You've been here before... but not this deep..."
4. **Every block opens with a question and closes with an answer** — the question is implicit, the answer earned
5. **Scripture sources must be cross-testamental** — no block pulls exclusively from OT or NT
6. **The Identity block (1) and Integration block (24) are mirror structures** — same number of days, same moods in reverse order

## 4. TRACT 3: THE CLOUD OF WITNESSES <a name="tract-3"></a>

**Subtitle:** "Walking with Those Who Walked Before"

### Design Philosophy

Tract 1 asks: *What did God do?*
Tract 2 asks: *What do YOU need?*
Tract 3 asks: *Who walked this road before you?*

This tract spends **extended time** with individual characters — not 1-3 days like Tract 1, but 10-21 days each. The user doesn't just learn about Moses. They walk with Moses. They sleep in his tent. They feel the sand under his feet. They hear his arguments with God. They watch him fail.

### The Handoff Principle

Each character "hands off" to the next. The last day of Moses' arc plants a seed for Joshua's. The last day of David's arc echoes forward to Jesus. Characters are not isolated units — they are a relay race carrying the same baton: the Master Mind.

### Bookend

**Day 1 — "Alone in the Garden"**
Scene: Adam. Alone. Before Eve, before sin — just after waking up. The first human consciousness. One man in an enormous world. The Master Mind insight: "Every story starts with one person and one God."

**Day 365 — "The Great Multitude"**
Scene: Revelation 7:9. Every character from the year is there. Moses, David, Ruth, Esther, Peter, Paul — all of them. And you. Not watching from outside — standing among them. The Master Mind insight: "You were never walking alone. They walked with you. And now you walk with those who come after."

## 5. CROSS-TRACT QUALITY RULES <a name="quality-rules"></a>

### Rules That Apply to All Three 365-Day Tracts

1. **No two tracts share the same bookend** — each opens and closes uniquely
2. **Scripture overlap is allowed** — the same passage in different tracts should yield a DIFFERENT PT Gem
3. **The same passage in Tract 1 vs. 2 vs. 3 must feel different:**
   - Tract 1 (Chronological): "Here is what happened next"
   - Tract 2 (Thematic): "Here is what this means for your struggle"
   - Tract 3 (Character): "Here is what this person felt"
4. **Depth tiers apply identically across all tracts** — Tier 1 (Days 1-30), Tier 2 (Days 31-120), Tier 3 (Days 121+)
5. **Sound design principles from NIGHT-WATCHES-SYSTEM.md apply to all tracts**
6. **Entry types, focus corrections, and healing specificity guide are shared resources** — all tracts draw from the same libraries
7. **No tract can be a subset of another** — if Tract 3 covers Moses for 21 days, those 21 days must differ from the Moses content in Tract 1's 7-day Moses block
8. **A user who completes all three tracts (1,095 sessions) should have no sense of repetition**

### Differentiation Test

For any given Scripture passage (e.g., Exodus 14 — the Red Sea):
- **Tract 1:** Sits at Day ~40 in a 7-session Moses block. It's one beat in a chronological sweep. PT Gem: "The Master Mind makes roads where there are none."
- **Tract 2:** Sits in Block 19 (Freedom, Day ~290). It's a metaphor for breaking what holds you — the Red Sea is YOUR Red Sea. PT Gem: "The sea you fear is the doorway to the life you want."
- **Tract 3:** Sits at Day 90 in a 21-session Moses block. The user has been with Moses for 14 days already — they've felt the plagues, the fear, the Pharaoh conversations. The sea is personal. PT Gem: "Moses didn't know it would part. He raised the staff anyway. Obedience is action in the absence of certainty."

Three passages. Same Scripture. Three completely different experiences.
